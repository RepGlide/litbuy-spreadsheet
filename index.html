Parcel Decision Ledger

Four Stages, Confirmed One at a Time

A spreadsheet row is an entry, not a decision. Work the four stages before you rely on any clue.

1Sheet cue

The spreadsheet only provides an entry or a clue. It points somewhere; it does not settle anything.

  • A row is a pointer, not a completed order.
  • Note where the entry came from.

2Source-page check

Re-open the page and re-read its options, size, price context, and the seller’s terms before proceeding.

  • Confirm the page matches the clue.
  • Re-read price against the selected option.

3Warehouse-photo observation

A photo describes only what is currently visible. It cannot prove authenticity or quality.

  • Match the photo to its stage.
  • Treat what is unseen as unproven.

4Parcel-prep variables

Weight, volume, packaging, restrictions, declaration, and destination rules are each confirmed separately.

  • No variable is assumed from the Sheet.
  • Confirm each one before submitting.
Source Link Check

Re-read the Source Page

✓Confirm on the page

  • The page title and domain match the clue.
  • The option, size, and version are selected.
  • The price is read in its option context.
  • The seller’s terms are visible.

✕Do not assume from the Sheet

  • That the Sheet price applies to your variant.
  • That the destination page is unchanged.
  • That seller terms stayed the same.
  • That a row is a confirmed listing.
Warehouse Photo Review

Listing Photo vs Warehouse Photo

Photo stageWhat it describesWhat it cannot prove
Listing photo The seller’s presentation of an item. That the item you receive will match.
Warehouse photo What is currently visible at a given moment. Authenticity, quality, or the final unit.

Parcel Variables

Confirm Each Variable Separately

No single number settles a parcel. Each variable is confirmed on its own before you submit.

Variables to confirm

  • Weight — measured, not estimated from the Sheet.
  • Volume — how the items pack together.
  • Packaging — materials and how items are grouped.
  • Restrictions — what the route will not carry.
  • Declaration — what is stated, not assumed.
  • Destination rules — the rules at the delivery end.

Pause Rules

When to Pause the Ledger

A pause is a decision, not a delay. These signals mean the ledger is not ready to move forward.

  • 1

    Source-page mismatch. The page title or domain does not match the clue. Pause

  • 2

    Option conflict. Size, color, version, or price no longer lines up with your selection. Pause

  • 3

    Photo gap. There is no warehouse or listing photo context for the item. Pause

  • 4

    Unconfirmed variable. Weight, volume, packaging, restrictions, declaration, or destination is still open. Pause

  • 5

    Untraceable entry. The row cannot be traced back to the Sheet or a category page. Pause

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. Each row is an entry or clue, not a completed order.

Is this website the official LitBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by LitBuy or Google.

Does the parcel decision ledger provide quotes or tracking?

No. The ledger is a reading framework. It does not provide shipping quotes, live tracking, order status, warehouse systems, or QC certification. Variables are confirmed separately on the source page.

How do I use the LitBuy spreadsheet to browse categories?

Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references, then use the category links on this page to browse a grouped catalog. Treat every row as an entry and confirm the source page before acting.

What should I check before submitting a parcel?

Confirm weight, volume, packaging, restrictions, declaration, and destination rules separately, and re-check the source page, options, size, price context, and seller terms before you rely on any listing clue.

Where do I place an order after reviewing a listing?

After reviewing a listing clue on the target page, you can continue through an agent shopping service to submit the item for processing. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
