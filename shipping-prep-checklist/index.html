Shipping Prep Checklist

The variables to confirm separately before submitting a parcel. This is a checklist, not a quote.

No single number settles a parcel

Shipping preparation is a set of separate variables. Each one is confirmed on its own, and none is assumed from the Sheet. This page does not provide shipping quotes or a live calculator.

Weight

Weight is measured, not estimated from a Sheet row. Different items and packaging change it, and it should be re-checked at the point of submission.

Volume

Volume is how the items pack together — the space a parcel occupies, not just its weight. Confirm how the selected items will actually be packed.

Packaging

Packaging covers materials and grouping: how items are wrapped, boxed, and combined. This affects both weight and volume.

Restrictions

Routes carry restrictions — items or materials a route will not carry. Confirm what the chosen route will and will not accept.

Declaration

Declaration is what is stated on the parcel, not what is assumed. Confirm what will be declared rather than leaving it open.

Destination rules

Destination rules are the requirements at the delivery end — duties, limits, and local conditions. Confirm them for your destination.

A worked reading

Consider a parcel with two pairs of sneakers and a jacket.

  1. Weight is measured after packaging, not read from a row.
  2. Volume depends on how the three items are boxed together.
  3. Restrictions may limit certain materials on the chosen route.
  4. Declaration and destination rules are confirmed for the delivery country.
